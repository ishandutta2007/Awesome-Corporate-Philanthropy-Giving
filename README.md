# Top Corporate Philanthropy & Giving Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  
*Focused on Employee Giving, Volunteer Management & Grant Lifecycle Automation*  
**Last updated: March 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Corporate Philanthropy & Giving**. These tools help companies manage employee matching gifts, volunteering programs, grants management, and impact reporting — enabling social impact programs at scale.

**Examples** include Salesforce Philanthropy Cloud, Benevity, Bright Funds, Deed, Submittable, YourCause by Blackbaud, Alaya by Benevity, Goodera, CyberGrants, and Millie (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: This section is heavily expanded with every major active project for self-hosting, custom volunteer management, and open grant lifecycle automation — ideal for nonprofits, foundations, and developers seeking transparent, vendor-independent giving platforms.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[Salesforce Philanthropy Cloud](https://www.salesforce.org/)**  
  Corporate impact platform created in partnership with United Way. Connects employees to 1.4M+ nonprofits with AI-driven personalized recommendations via Salesforce Einstein. Features giving, volunteering, campaign management, and real-time impact reporting.

- **[Benevity](https://benevity.com/)**  
  Enterprise Impact Platform unifying employee giving, volunteering, grants management, and ERG tools. Built on nearly two decades of compliance infrastructure: continuous validation of 2.5M nonprofits, fraud detection, automated sanctions screening, and global disbursement in 200+ countries. AI-native with Match Assurance and AI Grant Summaries.

- **[Bright Funds (Submittable)](https://www.yourcause.com/)**  
  Workplace giving and volunteering platform by WizeHive/Submittable. Features intuitive dashboard, donation matching, volunteer tracking, curated funds, and impact reporting. Microsoft Teams integration available.

- **[Deed](https://www.deed.com/)**  
  Corporate giving and volunteering platform enabling employees to donate, volunteer, and match gifts. Focuses on streamlined user experience and social impact engagement.

- **[Submittable](https://www.submittable.com/)**  
  Flexible submission management platform for grants, awards, scholarships, and contests. Features multi-round review workflows, concealed review for bias reduction, reviewer voting/feedback, and fund distribution to applicants.

- **[YourCause by Blackbaud](https://www.yourcause.com/)**  
  CSR platform with integrated grantmaking and employee engagement. Features 7.5+ million global nonprofits, 26 languages, generative-AI tools for CSR professionals, and 60+ standards reports. SOC1 and SOC2 Type 2 certified.

- **[Alaya by Benevity](https://benevity.com/)**  
  Employee engagement platform being unified into Benevity's primary Employee Engagement product. Alaya will sunset on December 15, 2026, with companies and employees migrating to Benevity.

- **[Goodera](https://www.goodera.com/)**  
  Corporate volunteering platform connecting employees to 50K+ nonprofits across 100+ countries. Hosts curated virtual, in-person, and hybrid experiences with full logistics management, 24/7 support, and impact reporting.

- **[CyberGrants](https://www.cybergrants.com/)**  
  Grant management system used by organizations including the NCUA for electronic grant and loan applications. Automates grant lifecycle workflows.

- **[Millie](https://www.millie.com/)**  
  Corporate giving and employee engagement platform focused on simplifying workplace philanthropy and volunteer programs.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects

- **[CiviCRM](https://github.com/reflexive-communications/civicrm-core)**  
  Leading open-source constituent relationship management system (AGPL-3.0) designed specifically for advocacy, nonprofit, and non-governmental organizations. Features donor management, contributions, memberships, events, grants, and volunteer management. Runs as standalone PHP or as a module within Backdrop, Drupal, Joomla, and WordPress. Coordinated by CiviCRM LLC with a global contributor community.

- **[Odoo Donation (OCA)](https://github.com/moduon/donation)**  
  Suite of donation management modules for Odoo (AGPL-3.0) maintained by the Odoo Community Association. Modules include: **donation** (core donation management), **donation_base** (base module), **donation_direct_debit** (auto-generate direct debit orders), **donation_recurring** (recurring donations), and **donation_sale** (manage donations in sale orders). Integrates with Odoo's CRM and accounting.

- **[OpenVolunteerPlatform](https://github.com/aerogear/openvolunteerplatform)**  
  Platform for building volunteer management systems (license varies) dedicated to local government and charity organizations. Started in March 2020 during COVID-19 to address isolation challenges. Provides customizable workforce management for volunteer coordination.

- **[NowCRM](https://github.com/nowtec-solutions/nowcrm)**  
  CRM platform for nonprofit organizations (AGPL-3.0) by Swiss company Nowtec Solutions. Born from Ukraine crisis response in 2022 when the team built a refugee housing management system. Features relationship management specifically designed for NGO needs. Self-hostable for maximum data control, with paid managed SaaS option including Swiss hosting.

- **[CiviCRM Extensions](https://github.com/civicrm)**  
  Extensive ecosystem of community-contributed extensions for CiviCRM. Includes modules for grant management, volunteer scheduling, donor receipts, payment processing (Stripe, PayPal), and integration with accounting systems.

- **[Odoo Nonprofit / NGO Modules](https://github.com/OCA)**  
  Odoo Community Association maintains numerous modules relevant to nonprofit operations including donation management, membership, event management, and volunteer coordination.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- **[SuiteCRM](https://github.com/salesagility/SuiteCRM)** — Open-source CRM (AGPL-3.0) adaptable for nonprofit donor management and grant tracking.
- **[EspoCRM](https://github.com/espocrm/espocrm)** — Lightweight open-source CRM with custom entities for volunteer and donor management.
- **[ERPNext Non-Profit](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)** — Open-source ERP (GPL-3.0) with donation management, membership, and grant tracking modules.
- **[Giveth](https://github.com/Giveth)** — Open-source blockchain-based giving platform for transparent charitable donations.
- **[DonorDrive](https://github.com/donordrive)** — Peer-to-peer fundraising platform components (license varies).
- Community **n8n** workflows for donor acknowledgment automation and volunteer scheduling.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Combine **CiviCRM** as the core nonprofit CRM with **Odoo Donation** modules for financial integration, **OpenVolunteerPlatform** for volunteer scheduling, and **PostgreSQL** for data persistence — creating a fully self-hosted, enterprise-grade corporate philanthropy platform.
